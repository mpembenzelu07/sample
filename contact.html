Frequently asked questions

How can I donate?

You can donate via bank transfer, mobile money, or at our office. All donations are receipted and go directly to surgical and vision programs.

What volunteer roles are open?

Medical professionals, community educators, admin support, and field assistants. Email volunteer@cavitrd.org for current openings.

How do I access surgical services?

Through our outreach camps or referrals. Contact our medical team at medical@cavitrd.org for eligibility and schedules.

Can my organization partner with CAVITRD?

Absolutely! We collaborate with NGOs, hospitals, and corporates. Reach out to partnerships@cavitrd.org.
