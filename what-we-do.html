Our Core Programs

Integrated care for children and adults with disabilities.

Medical

Surgical Outreach Camps

Mobile surgical camps in remote areas provide free, life-changing procedures for congenital anomalies and trauma-related disabilities.

  • Free congenital anomaly surgeries
  • Trauma & post-operative care
  • Follow-up rehabilitation
Vision

Vision Impairment Support

Comprehensive vision screening, treatment, and assistive devices to restore sight and improve quality of life.

  • Community vision screening
  • Prescription glasses & low vision aids
  • Rehabilitation & referral
Education

Community Education

Awareness programs on prevention, early detection, and available treatments for disabilities.

  • Prevention campaigns
  • Early detection workshops
  • Treatment option education

Surgical Outreach Program

We bring skilled surgical teams to remote communities, performing essential operations for children and adults who would otherwise never receive care. Our focus: congenital anomalies (cleft lip, clubfoot) and trauma-related disabilities.
