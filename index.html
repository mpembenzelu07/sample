With Compassion, We Rise

Bringing expert care to rural Tanzania since 2017.

CAVITRD (Registration No. 14NGO/R/4896) is dedicated to children and adults facing congenital anomalies, vision loss, and trauma-related disabilities. Through surgical camps and awareness, we bring hope to remote villages in the Lake Zone.

Mobile surgical camps
Life-changing operations in underserved regions
Vision restoration
Screening, glasses, and specialist referrals
Preventive education
Awareness about trauma prevention & nutrition

Our Path Forward

Driven by purpose, guided by love

Vision

A world where no child suffers from treatable disabilities due to lack of access — building an inclusive Tanzania.

Mission

Deliver sustainable, high-quality surgical & rehabilitative care to rural communities through outreach and advocacy.

Core Objectives

Raise awareness, reduce preventable disabilities, provide treatment & empower communities with knowledge.
