Who We Are

CAVITRD is a registered Tanzanian NGO with Registration number 14NGO/R/4896, dealing with children and adults with congenital or acquired anomalies, vision impairment, and trauma-related disabilities.

We work with rural communities, especially across the Lake Zone region of Tanzania. Through surgical camps, vision care, and preventive education, we create lasting change.

Every life restored brings happiness to families and entire villages.

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

Provision of surgical treatment to children born with congenital anomalies and those who acquire trauma-related disabilities. We also facilitate education to communities to prevent disabilities.

Our Mission

Provision of quality health care services and education to rural areas through organized surgical camps and outreach programs in the Lake Zone regions.

Our Core Objectives

Enhancing Awareness

Enhancing society's awareness of available treatment options for congenital anomalies and trauma-related disabilities. Informed communities lead to empowered families.

Advocating Prevention

Advocating for prevention and protection measures to reduce the development of anomalies and trauma-related disabilities through education and early intervention.

Our Core Values

Dignity

Respecting every individual's humanity and inherent worth.

Equity

Compassion
